# Myfosa Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 23, 2026
**Version:** 1.0

## In short

Myfosa translates text **on your device**. Everything you type, speak or photograph is processed locally and is not sent to the developer's servers. The app has no accounts, ads or analytics, and we do not receive your text, voice, photos or translation history.

## 1. Who we are

The Myfosa App is developed by SiaSoft.
For privacy questions, contact us at: [vyloqra@gmail.com].

## 2. What data the App processes

All data listed in this section is processed on your device only.

| What | Why | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Text you type or dictate, and its translation | To perform the translation | In device memory while the App runs; see "History" below |
| Translation history (source text, translation, languages, time) | So you can return to earlier translations | A local database inside the App on your device |
| Images from the camera or photo library | To recognize text in them and translate it | In memory only, while being processed. The App does not save your photos |
| Settings (theme, model unload timeout, onboarding completed flag) | So the App works the way you configured it | Locally on your device |
| The translation model file and technical information about it (name, version, size) | To translate without an internet connection | Locally on your device |

**History.** Translations from the current session are added to the permanent history when you send the App to the background. You can delete a single entry or clear the whole history on the History screen. Deletion is permanent.

## 3. What we do not do

- We do not collect or send to our servers your text, audio, images or translation history.
- We do not create accounts or ask for your email, phone number or name.
- We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.
- We do not use third-party SDKs for analytics or statistics.
- We do not sell your data or share it with third parties.

## 4. Network requests

The App uses the internet for two purposes only:

1. **Configuration check.** The App requests a small configuration file (model version, download address, minimum App version). It is hosted on Hugging Face. The last configuration received is saved on your device.
2. **Downloading and updating the translation model.** The model file is downloaded from the address given in the configuration. Once it is downloaded, translation works offline.

These requests **do not contain** your text, history or any other user data. As with any website request, the server that answers can technically see the device's IP address and connection metadata. How such a service handles that information is governed by its own privacy policy. We do not have access to it and do not link it to you.

## 5. Permissions

The App asks only for the access a specific feature needs. You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings → Myfosa.

- **Camera.** To photograph text and translate it. Text recognition on the image runs on the device.
- **Microphone and speech recognition.** For voice input. The App asks iOS to recognize speech **on the device** when this is supported for the selected language. If it is not supported for a language, iOS may send the audio to Apple's servers for recognition. In that case Apple's rules apply, not the App's, and we have no access to the recording.
- **Photos.** When you pick an image from your library, the App receives only that image, not your entire photo library.

Reading translations aloud uses the built-in iOS speech synthesizer.

## 6. Clipboard

When you tap "Copy", the translation is placed on the iOS clipboard. Other apps may read it according to system rules. We do not read the clipboard and never put anything into it without your action.

## 7. Storage, deletion and backups

- Data is stored on your device until you delete it or delete the App.
- **History** can be deleted one entry at a time or all at once on the History screen.
- **The model** can be deleted under Settings → Delete model. The model file is excluded from backups.
- **Deleting the App** erases all of its local data.
- The local history database may be included in a device backup that you create with Apple's tools (iCloud or a computer). Such backups are under your control and Apple's rules; we have no access to them. If you do not want your history to end up in a backup, clear it in the App.

## 8. Security

Because your data stays on your device, its protection depends first of all on the protection of the device itself: passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and iOS updates. When the model is downloaded, its integrity is verified (size and checksum) if they are provided in the configuration.

## 9. Children

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. All data stays on the device.

## 10. Your rights

We do not receive or store your personal data, so there is nothing for us to provide, correct or delete. You control all content in the App yourself (see Section 7). If you nevertheless believe that we are processing your data, write to us at [vyloqra@gmail.com]. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law, including the GDPR and the personal data laws of your country.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when new features are added. The current version is always available in the App and in this repository. The effective date at the top shows when the policy was last changed. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the App. We will not start collecting data without your consent.
